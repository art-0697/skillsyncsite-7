Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SkillSync.

What is SkillSync?

SkillSync is a platform designed to connect individuals who want to learn new skills with those who are willing to teach or exchange their expertise. It fosters a community of collaborative learning and growth.

How do I create a profile?

Creating a profile on SkillSync is simple! Click on the "Create My Profile Now" button in the "Ready to Elevate Your Skills?" section. You'll be guided through a quick registration process where you can highlight your skills, what you're offering, and what you're seeking to learn.

Is SkillSync free to use?

Yes, basic membership and skill exchange on SkillSync are absolutely free! We believe in fostering a community where knowledge can be shared freely. Future premium features may be introduced, but the core functionality will remain free.

How do I connect with other members?

Once you've created your profile, you can browse through other member profiles in the "Browse Talent" section. Each profile has a "View Profile" button which will allow you to see more details and initiate a connection or message request directly to discuss a skill exchange.
